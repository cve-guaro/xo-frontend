# XO Ethiopia logo kit

## What is included

This kit keeps the supplied XO symbol and ET submark recognizable, with full-color, black, white, grayscale, light-background, dark-background, horizontal, symbol-only, rounded-square, and circular app/avatar versions. SVG files are scalable; PNG files are pre-rendered at practical sizes.

## Reference palette

| Role | Color | Hex |

| X — crimson | Sampled from the clean supplied logo | `#FC073A` |

These are practical digital approximations sampled from compressed JPG references, not confirmed official print specifications or Pantone values. For exact production matching, use the original source artwork or verify against the brand owner’s master colors.

## Which version to use

- **Full-color transparent**: the primary stacked lockup for light backgrounds; ET is charcoal.
- **Full-color dark background**: use as supplied on dark navy; ET is white.
- **Black / white monochrome**: one-ink printing, stamps, embroidery, or high-contrast placements. Use black on light surfaces and white on dark surfaces.
- **Grayscale**: neutral applications where color is unavailable or inappropriate.
- **Horizontal lockup**: a compact alternative for headers, narrow banners, and footer areas.
- **XO-only symbol**: app icons, avatars, favicons, and small spaces. Prefer this over the full lockup when the ET submark would become hard to read.
- **Rounded-square / circle / white-square icon tiles**: ready-to-use app or profile images; use only where a contained icon is appropriate.

## Basic usage rules (recommended)

Keep the logo’s proportions; never stretch or skew it. Leave clear space around the mark of at least half the height of the XO symbol. Use the full lockup at 140 px wide or larger on screen and about 30 mm wide or larger in print; for smaller uses switch to the XO-only icon (recommended minimum 24 px / 8 mm). Use the SVG for print and large layouts, and PNG for apps and messaging. Avoid placing the charcoal ET version on dark backgrounds or the white version on light backgrounds.

The source artwork’s blue and red edges have been traced from the clean reference; the more legible white ET submark was traced from the second, glowing reference. Glow is intentionally not part of the core assets: keep effects out of official or print uses.
